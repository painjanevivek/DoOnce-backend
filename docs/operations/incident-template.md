- Started / detected / last updated (UTC):
- Incident commander and technical lead:
- Affected capability and regions:
- User-visible symptoms:
- Known scope (aggregate tenants/runs only):
- Stable error/event codes:
- Current containment:
- Data confidentiality or integrity impact: confirmed / suspected / none observed:
- Next decision and update time:

## Customer update

> We are investigating an issue affecting **[capability]** beginning at **[UTC time]**. **[User-visible impact in plain language]**. Existing **[unaffected capabilities]** continue to operate. We have **[containment or mitigation]** and will provide the next update by **[UTC time]**.

Do not include tenant names, workflow definitions, page content, credentials, tokens, screenshots, or speculation. After resolution, publish the cause, affected window, verified impact, corrective actions, and prevention work at the appropriate level of detail.
