# Pricing and Packaging Validation

Pricing is a decision after customer evidence, not a number to invent in source code.

## Inputs to collect

- verified outcomes and the participant's manual baseline;
- authoring/model cost;
- hosted-browser time and cost;
- artifact storage/transfer cost;
- support time and loaded support cost;
- failure investigation and repair cost;
- observed willingness to pay and actual pilot revenue, if any.

Use `calculateUnitEconomics` only with one currency and the same observation period. It reports variable cost per verified outcome and, when observed revenue exists, observed contribution margin. It does not select a price or estimate “time saved.”

## Interview decision record

For each target buyer, record task frequency, definition of done, current workaround, budget owner, procurement constraints, acceptable failure/review model, and willingness to pay. Remove names and confidential task data from repository evidence.

Choose quotas for authoring, hosted minutes, storage, runs, and support only after the cost distribution is visible. Keep billing disabled until the package, refund/cancellation policy, tax handling, and legal terms are approved.
