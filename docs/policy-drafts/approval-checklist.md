# Policy Approval Checklist

This checklist records prerequisites; it is not legal approval.

- Legal entity, registered address, operating jurisdiction, and launch markets are confirmed.
- Privacy/data-protection and security contacts are staffed.
- Actual data categories, purposes, transfers, processors, locations, retention, deletion, and backups match product behavior.
- Chrome Web Store disclosures, consent UI, privacy notice, and Limited Use statements agree.
- Terms cover the narrow supported use, user responsibilities, prohibited tasks, availability, suspension, and artifact responsibility.
- Provider contracts and subprocessor/data-retention terms are reviewed.
- DPA, cross-border transfer, consumer, tax, and e-signature needs are assessed for the launch scope.
- Approved documents have version, approver, approval date, effective date, and publication location.

Until qualified counsel completes this list, public pages remain labelled pre-launch and `legal-policy-approval` remains pending in the launch manifest.
