## Intended service boundary

DoOnce assists with reviewed browser workflows; it is not an unrestricted browser agent. It must pause rather than guess when a page changes, a workflow is uncertain, or an action requires a prohibited or unapproved category.

The public terms must make clear that users remain responsible for:

- ensuring they are authorised to access each supported website and use the workflow;
- reviewing a workflow before activation and every preview before any supported run;
- keeping account credentials secure and not using DoOnce to bypass access controls, CAPTCHA, OTP, or site terms; and
- validating downloaded reports and any resulting business decision.

## Explicit exclusions to preserve

The service does not support payments, fund transfers, final submissions, account changes, credential entry or storage, OTP handling, CAPTCHA bypass, or autonomous high-impact actions. DoOnce may pause, disable, or remove a workflow when safety, reliability, or legal risk requires it.
