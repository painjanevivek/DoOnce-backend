# DoOnce policy drafts

These are internal, pre-launch source drafts. They are not legal advice, contractual terms, or final public policies. The dashboard provides clearly labelled, non-binding pre-launch summaries at `/privacy` and `/terms` so visitors can understand the product boundary. Product launch remains blocked until counsel has completed the bracketed placeholders, confirmed the applicable jurisdictions, and approved final published versions.

The drafts reflect the product boundary: DoOnce automates only reviewed low-risk browser workflows and must never record or process passwords, OTPs, payment credentials, final submissions, or other prohibited actions.

## Review checklist

- Confirm the legal entity, registered address, support contact, and data-protection contact.
- Confirm hosting, subprocessors, cross-border transfers, and retention periods.
- Confirm applicable privacy, consumer, and e-signature laws for every launch market.
- Publish approved versions in the product before collecting production personal data.
- Record approval date, version, counsel, and effective date for every published policy.

## Public draft summaries

The frontend summaries must remain consistent with these source drafts and must clearly state that DoOnce is pre-launch. Do not add a checkbox that records legal acceptance, claim an effective date, or present placeholder contact, retention, subprocessor, or jurisdiction details as final until counsel approves them.

Useful references: the [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf) and the European Commission’s [privacy-notice guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/rules-business-and-organisations/principles-gdpr/what-information-must-be-given-individuals-whose-data-collected_en).
